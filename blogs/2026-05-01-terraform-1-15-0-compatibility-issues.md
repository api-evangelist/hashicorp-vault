Status: Resolved Our Engineering team has identified a number of critical problems with Terraform 1.15.0 and certain configurations. Terraform 1.15.1 was released today with fixes for known critical issues. If you are experiencing issues with 1.15.0, we recommend you either upgrade to 1.15.1, or downgrade to 1.14.9.
